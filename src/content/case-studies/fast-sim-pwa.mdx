## The constraints

[TWO OR THREE THINGS THAT GENUINELY SHAPED THE BUILD. Real candidates:

- **The device is whatever the agent already owns.** Mid-range Android, limited
  storage, no possibility of mandating hardware — hence a PWA rather than a
  native app they'd have to install from a store and keep updated.
- **Two product lines, one flow.** Physical SIM and eSIM activate differently
  but agents shouldn't have to learn two apps.
- **Every screen is scoped to a role.** An agent, a dealer and head office see
  materially different things from the same data.]

<Note title="Why a PWA">
  [EXPLAIN THE CHOICE PROPERLY: install without an app store, ship a fix the

### Filtering and reporting at real volume

[THIS IS THE INTERESTING PART — GO DEEP.

### Role-based rendering

The interface never offers an action the API is going to refuse.

[EXPLAIN THE MECHANISM: where do permissions come from — the token, a

And the important part: **the UI is a convenience, not the enforcement.** Say
