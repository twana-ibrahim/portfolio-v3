## The problem

Fastlink sells SIM and eSIM cards through a network of field agents — people
working out of small shops and market stalls across the region, not out of an
office with a fixed line. [DESCRIBE THE BEFORE STATE: what was the process?

## The constraints

Three things shaped every decision on this project:

- **Connectivity is not a given.** [DESCRIBE: intermittent mobile data, agents
  moving between coverage areas, sales happening in places with no signal at all.]
- **The device is whatever the agent already owns.** [DESCRIBE: mid-range and
  older Android handsets, limited storage, no possibility of mandating hardware.]
- **Activations are financial transactions.** A duplicate submission or a lost
  record is not a cosmetic bug — it is stock that cannot be reconciled.
